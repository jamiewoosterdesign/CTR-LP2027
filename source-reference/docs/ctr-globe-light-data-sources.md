# CTR light concept: data sources

Checked 22 September 2026. Applies only to /concept-globe-light/.

2027 design placeholders: OTA research 30%; superior/luxury rooms 62%; increased likelihood of event travel 67%; willingness to pay more to reduce overtourism 46%. All changes (+4 percentage points) are illustrative. These are existing 2026 measures; repeat inclusion and comparable wording/populations in 2027 are not confirmed.

2026 global baselines: 26%, 58%, 63%, 42% respectively.
- Discovery: https://www.siteminder.com/news/changing-traveller-report-2026/ (research starting points: OTA 26, search 21, word of mouth 14, familiar brands 7, AI 4; selected channels, not complete distribution).
- Premium rooms: https://www.siteminder.com/r/latest-trends-in-hotel-industry/ (global 58; selected market non-standard/premium preferences: China 84, Indonesia 74, India 71, Australia 60, France 56).
- Events: https://www.siteminder.com/changing-traveller-report/ (global 63). Original chart: https://www.siteminder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Graph-The-Expanding-Value-Mindset-3-of-3.svg (Gen Z 81, Millennials 74, Gen X 54, Boomers 36, Radio babies 32).
- Overtourism: same report and https://www.siteminder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Graph-The-Expanding-Value-Mindset-2-of-3.svg (42 willing; 24 destination-dependent; 34 refuse). Do not conflate the first two categories. Narrative notes guarantees/conditions among willing respondents.
